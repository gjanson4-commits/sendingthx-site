Try it as a florist

See the whole delivery on your own phone in about two minutes. You play the customer who sends the flowers and the driver who delivers them — the recipient can be you or a coworker.

  1. Create a demo delivery below.
  2. Open sendingthx.com/driver and sign in with your mobile number and the code we text you.
  3. Tap the delivery, take a photo of any flowers or plant, and tap Delivered.
  4. Watch the texts arrive — the sender gets the photo, the recipient gets it with a way to say thanks. Reply to see the thank-you come back.

💡 Testing alone? Use your own name and number for the recipient too — both texts come to your phone.
